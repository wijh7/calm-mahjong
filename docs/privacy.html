Privacy Policy — Calm Mahjong

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Contact: support.wijh7@gmail.com

Calm Mahjong ("the app") is a single-player tile-matching game. We built it to be quiet and respectful, so we collect as little as possible. This policy explains what the app stores, what third parties may process, and your choices.

1. What we do NOT do

  • No account, no login, no sign-up.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • We run no analytics or tracking SDK of our own, and we do not sell your data.

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress (level, coins, garden items, settings such as language, sound and text size, streak, and whether you own Calm Pass) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it. Android's backup feature may copy this data to your own Google account if you have device backup turned on; that is controlled by you in Android settings. Uninstalling the app deletes the local data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are shown only when you tap to watch one, and other ad formats follow the limits described in the app. When ads are requested, Google may collect and process the following, according to Google's own policies: your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, for serving ads, measuring them, and preventing fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, the app first shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) and requests ads according to your choice. You can change that choice later in Settings. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. Calm Pass (one-time purchase)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. The app only learns, through Google Play, whether Calm Pass is owned so it can remove ads and unlock extras. If a purchase is refunded, access ends.

5. Notifications

If you choose to turn on the gentle reminder, the app schedules one local notification on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

6. Sharing

If you tap Share on the daily result, the app opens the Android share sheet with a short text, and you choose where it goes. Nothing is shared unless you tap it.

7. Children

Calm Mahjong is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your personal data on our own servers, most of this is done on your device (clear app data or uninstall) and in your Google account and Android ad settings. For anything else, email us.

9. Security

Local data stays in the app's private storage on your device. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
